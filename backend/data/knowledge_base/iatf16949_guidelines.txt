IATF 16949 is the global technical specification and quality management standard for the automotive industry.
It strongly emphasizes defect prevention, reduction of variation, and waste reduction in the supply chain.

Key requirements include the Production Part Approval Process (PPAP), which ensures that suppliers understand all engineering design records and specification requirements.
A Control Plan is mandatory, describing the systems used to minimize process and product variation. It must cover all phases: prototype, pre-launch, and production.
Measurement System Analysis (MSA) is required to analyze the variation present in measurement and test equipment. Gage R&R (Repeatability and Reproducibility) studies are critical here.

Advanced Product Quality Planning (APQP) phases must be followed to ensure new products satisfy the customer.
Supplier quality management requires organizations to cascade IATF 16949 requirements down the supply chain.
Organizations must also adhere to Customer-Specific Requirements (CSRs) from OEMs such as BMW, VW, and Mercedes-Benz, which may dictate specific inspection frequencies, automated optical inspection (AOI) standards, and documentation formats for defect logging.
