In German manufacturing, identifying the root cause of specific defects is essential for maintaining high OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness).

Scratch (Kratzer) Causes:
- Abrasive contact with handling equipment or conveyor belts.
- Tool wear in machining centers.
- Improper manual handling by operators.
Prevention: Regular maintenance of conveyor guides, implementation of soft-touch handling materials, and strict tool life management.

Crack (Haarriss) Causes:
- Thermal stress from uneven cooling in casting or forging.
- Material fatigue due to excessive vibration.
- Overloading during assembly processes.
Prevention: Controlled cooling environments, vibration dampening, and calibrated torque tools. Detection often requires dye penetrant or ultrasonic inspection. Cost impact is high.

Dent (Delle) Causes:
- Excessive fixture pressure during clamping.
- Handling impacts or drops.
- Foreign objects in stamping dies.
Prevention: Calibrated pneumatic clamps, automated handling systems, and clean-die protocols.

Porosity (Porosität) Causes:
- Gas entrapment during die casting.
- Incorrect die temperature.
- Injection speed too high or low.
Prevention: Optimization of gating design, thermal imaging of dies, and vacuum-assisted casting.

Corrosion (Korrosion) Causes:
- High ambient humidity in the facility.
- Chemical exposure from cutting fluids.
- Surface treatment (e.g., anodizing, plating) failure.
Prevention: Climate-controlled storage, regular fluid chemistry checks, and strict adherence to plating bath parameters.

Inclusion (Einschluss) Causes:
- Contamination in raw materials.
- Material impurity.
- Die wear flaking into the part.
Prevention: Raw material certification, melt filtration, and predictive die maintenance.
